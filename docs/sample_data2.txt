RAG stands for Retrieval Augmented Generation, an AI technique.
In RAG, AI finds answers from external documents instead of training data.
The three main steps of RAG are Retrieve, Augment and Generate.

Vector Database stores text as numbers called vectors.
ChromaDB, Pinecone and FAISS are popular vector databases.
LangChain is a Python framework for building AI applications.

Embedding model converts text into numerical representation.
all-MiniLM-L6-v2 is a popular free embedding model from HuggingFace.
OpenAI GPT-4 and Anthropic Claude are popular LLM models.
